Defaults match the PR: a block counts when the chance is above 0.5 and it's louder than −55 dBFS; a pause drains the count by 16 ms a block; about 0.4 s passes. “Hi,” lights at 128 ms. Background is the median level of blocks the detector didn't take for speech. The live mic asks for echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain off, as Teddy does, and “Processing off” shows whether the browser agreed. If the microphone is refused here, upload a recording instead.
